Embedded contract recruiting

More recruiting capacity. Without another full-time hire.

When you have more open roles than recruiting capacity, paying an agency fee on every hire gets expensive fast. Pio embeds directly with your team instead — working in your systems, representing your company, and managing your openings as an extension of your recruiting function.

What embedded looks like

  • We work inside your team — your email, your ATS, your careers page, and your voice with candidates
  • Full-cycle recruiting on your openings — sourcing, screening, scheduling, candidate communication, and support through offer
  • Multiple roles worked in parallel — prioritized alongside your hiring managers based on what the business needs most
  • Senior-level recruiting support — hands-on recruiters with real talent acquisition leadership behind them, not junior staff working from a script
  • Flexible capacity — scale support up during a hiring push and back down as positions close
  • Everything stays with you — pipelines, candidate relationships, notes, processes, and market intelligence remain in your systems

This fits you if…

  • Your team is making several hires and doesn't have the recruiting capacity to keep up
  • You have an internal recruiter, but a hiring surge is more than one person can carry
  • You've looked at agency fees across several hires and need a more efficient alternative
  • You need experienced recruiting support but aren't ready to add permanent recruiting headcount
  • You want someone who can recruit and spot opportunities to improve the way you're hiring

More than an extra set of hands.

Embedded recruiting or contingent search?

Both are real solutions — to different problems. Here's how they compare.

Embedded recruiting Contingent search
Best for Multiple open roles One or a few difficult roles
Works as Part of your team External search partner

Common questions

How is embedded recruiting different from using an agency?

An agency works externally to fill a specific role and typically earns a placement fee for each hire. With embedded recruiting, Pio works as part of your team across multiple openings for a defined period or level of support. We work in your systems, represent your employer brand, and build pipelines that stay with you.

Who actually does the recruiting?

Your engagement is delivered by senior, hands-on recruiters with real talent acquisition leadership behind them — not work sold by a senior consultant and handed off to a junior recruiter.

Do candidates know you're an outside recruiter?

We represent your company consistently and transparently, using your systems, processes, and employer brand. The goal is for candidates to receive the same experience they'd expect from a strong internal recruiting function.

What if our hiring slows down?

That's the point of the model. Embedded support is designed to flex with your hiring needs rather than requiring you to add permanent recruiting headcount.
