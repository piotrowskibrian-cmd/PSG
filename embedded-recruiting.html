Common questions

How is embedded recruiting different from using an agency?

An agency charges a placement fee — often 20% or more of salary — on every single hire. An embedded recruiter works as part of your team for a flat engagement, working as many reqs as capacity allows. If you're making multiple hires, the math strongly favors embedded.

Do candidates know you're not our employee?

We work under your banner — your email, your careers page, your voice. Candidates experience your company, not an agency. How you introduce us internally and externally is your choice, and we follow it.

What if our hiring slows down mid-engagement?

The engagement scales with your hiring. Scale hours down when reqs close, back up when a new round of hiring starts, or pause entirely between pushes. That flexibility is the point of the model.
