About Pio Services Group

Pio Services Group is a fractional recruiting and contingent search firm built on a simple idea: the engagement should fit the problem, not the other way around. Traditional agencies have one product — a placement fee on every hire — whether that's what you need or not.

We work differently. We've helped companies build and develop talent acquisition teams from the ground up, implement recruiting technology, and shape their employer brand. We've embedded with hiring teams to work their reqs like our own. And when a company just needs one hard role filled, we've taken the job order and delivered the hire.

Whatever's missing from your hiring picture, we're built to fit it.
