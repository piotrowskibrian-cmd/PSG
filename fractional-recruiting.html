Common questions

What is fractional recruiting?

Fractional recruiting means getting senior recruiting leadership and hands-on execution without a full-time hire. We work a set portion of our time inside your business — building your talent acquisition process, tools, and pipelines — at a fraction of the cost of a full-time TA leader.

Do you help us hire our own recruiters?

Yes. Part of building a TA function is often hiring the people who will run it. We can define the roles, recruit your recruiters, and get them up to speed on the process and tools we've built together.

What happens when the build-out is done?

Your call. Some companies take the keys and run everything in-house. Others keep us on fractionally for leadership, surge capacity, or hard searches. The goal is a function that works without depending on us.
